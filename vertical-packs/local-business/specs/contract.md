# Local business site: what this kind of product is

Read this first. This pack describes a one page website for a local business, with the restaurant as its first kind. It is not an online store, not a waitlist and not an app. Where any instruction or example says "store" or "shopper", read "site" and "visitor".

## What it is

A home page made of sections in a default order: hero, menu highlights, full menu, about, gallery, reviews, catering, ways to enjoy us, footer. Only the sections that have real content are drawn. There is no database, no owner area, no sign-up form and no email. The page's words are not edited in a screen: the owner asks the builder (Scout) to change them.

## Who it is for

A neighbourhood restaurant owner who already has an old or messy website, or none, and wants a clean page that states what they already say about themselves: the menu with prices, hours, address, phone, how to book or order. Usually busy and not technical.

## Where the content comes from

The brief is written from the owner's existing website (read by the website reader) and from the interview. The reader copies facts and words exactly as the owner's site has them. This pack's rule is the reader's rule: **nothing is invented.** If something was not found, the section is left out and the review list says what is missing.

## What good looks like

- A visitor sees the name, what kind of food it is, when it is open, and one clear way to book or call, within five seconds on a phone.
- The full menu is easy to scan: categories, dishes and prices, exactly as the owner wrote them.
- The page looks like design restaurant_001: clean, friendly, bright, one accent colour, soft cards.
- The page uses the owner's pictures only after the owner confirms they own the site and its pictures. Before that it uses labelled colour blocks and still looks designed.

## What it is not

No online ordering or payments, no table booking system or form, no visitor accounts, no database, no blog, no multi-page site, no analytics, no page editor, no reviews written by the builder, no stock photos.

## Principles

- The design is the reference in `reference/`. The agent copies it and fills it from the brief; it does not redesign it.
- Text from the owner's site is copied, not improved. Spelling, capitals and punctuation stay.
- A section with nothing real to show is not drawn. A button with nowhere to go is not drawn.
- The brief decides the words, the sections and the accent. These specs decide how everything behaves.
- When the brief is silent, choose a simple default and list it in AGENT_LOG.md under "Decisions on ambiguous points".
- Quiet by default: no pop-ups, no auto-playing media, no fake urgency.
