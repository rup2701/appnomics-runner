# Interview step: choosing a direction

DRAFT. When wired in, this becomes a short step after the brand question (tone and colour) and before the buttons.

## What Scout does

Scout does not ask five design questions. It proposes **three named directions** in plain words, built from the product, the audience, the tone and the colour the owner already gave. Each is one line, with the feeling first.

Example, for a scheduling tool with a bold tone:

> I have three directions in mind for your page. Which feels closest?
>
> 1. **Planning board.** Crisp and grid-led, with small status dots and technical labels. Feels like a tool for people who like to be in control.
> 2. **Quiet editorial.** Serif headlines and a lot of air. Feels calm and considered.
> 3. **Soft and friendly.** Rounded shapes and a gentle glow. Feels approachable.
>
> Pick one, or tell me what to change, for example "the first one but warmer".

## Rules

- Exactly three options, each different in typography and motif, never three variations of one idea. All three must suit the tone the owner chose; a serious product is never offered a playful direction.
- Describe the feeling and the look in everyday words. Never name fonts, hex codes, pixel sizes or em values to the owner.
- Behind each option Scout holds the matching `creative` block from `creative.md` (pairing, motif, hero kind, signature elements).
- The owner may also say a reference ("something like Linear"). Scout notes it as a reference, writes what it takes from it in everyday words, and still shows the three directions.
- The hero question follows in one line: "How should we show your product at the top? A framed screenshot, a small piece of the interface we draw for you, a diagram, big words only, or your own illustration?" If the owner picks their own illustration, Scout asks them to attach it (see `uploads.md`) or marks it as a placeholder to fix.
- On the review screen, show the chosen direction as one plain line ("Planning board: crisp, grid-led, technical labels") in the list of what was decided.
- If the owner skips the step, use the tone's default direction from `creative.md` and say which one on the review.
