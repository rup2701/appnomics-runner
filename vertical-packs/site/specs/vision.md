# Waitlist site: what this kind of product is

Read this first. This pack describes a one-page website that tests an idea by collecting sign-ups. It is not an online store. Wherever the builder's instructions say "store" or "shopper", read "site" and "visitor".

## What it is

A single public page made of blocks in a default order (hero, product preview, benefits, waitlist form, footer), plus a small owner area where the owner reads the sign-ups and edits the words on the page.

## Who it is for

A founder with an idea who wants to know within days whether anyone cares, without spending a day on design, hosting and a sign-up database. Usually technical enough to judge the result, and short of time.

## What good looks like

- A visitor understands the promise in about five seconds and can join in under a minute, on a phone.
- The page looks credible: restrained, readable, well spaced, with one clear call to action.
- Every sign-up is stored once, with the answers to the form's questions and where the visitor came from.
- The owner sees at a glance how many people joined, progress toward their goal, which answers are most common, and can download the list.
- Nothing on the page is invented. Claims, numbers, testimonials and product screenshots come from the owner, or are left out, or are labelled as a concept.

## What it is not

No payments, no visitor accounts, no sending email in this version, no blog, no multi-page marketing site, no analytics suite. One page, one goal.

## Principles

- The page is blocks in an order, not a free canvas. The owner can hide a block and edit its words and images. Structure changes are change requests.
- The brief decides the words, the look and the form. These specs decide how everything behaves.
- When the brief is silent, choose a simple default and list it in AGENT_LOG.md under "Decisions on ambiguous points" so the owner can review it.
- Quiet by default: no pop-ups, no auto-playing media, no fake urgency.
