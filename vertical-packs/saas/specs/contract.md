# SaaS product showcase: what this kind of product is

Read this first. This pack describes a one-page showcase for a software product. It is not an online store, not a waitlist and not an app. Where any instruction or example says "store" or "shopper", read "site" and "visitor".

## What it is

A single public page made of sections in a default order: hero with the main button and a product image, benefits (a bento grid), how it works, pricing, testimonials, FAQ, a closing call to action, and a full footer. There is no sign-up form, no database, no owner area and no email. The main button is a link the owner sets (a sign-up page, a booking page, a mailto), and until they do it scrolls to the closing section and is flagged "Set your link". The page's words are not edited in a screen: changes are made by asking the builder (Scout).

Optional sections the owner can switch on: examples gallery, comparison table, use-case cards.

## Who it is for

A founder or small team that needs a credible product page fast, in the style of good software and API sites, without a designer. Usually technical enough to judge the result and short of time. The owner reviews and fixes the draft; the builder does not pretend the draft is final.

## What good looks like

- A visitor understands what the product does in about five seconds and sees one clear main button, on a phone.
- The page looks like a real company: spacious, one clear accent, large type, soft cards, a real footer with Product, Company, Resources, Privacy, Terms and Security links.
- The brand is right: tone, color and words match what the owner chose in the interview.
- Anything that is not real yet (pricing, testimonials, the product image, links with no page) is visibly marked as sample, and is listed in the brief's `placeholders` so the owner knows what to check.

## What it is not

No sign-up or email capture, no sending email, no payments or checkout, no visitor accounts, no database, no blog, no multi-page site, no analytics, no page editor, no legal text written by the builder.

## Principles

- The page is sections in an order. A section the owner switched off is not on the page or in the navigation.
- Drafted copy (headline, benefits, steps, FAQ) is written from the interview answers. Sample content (testimonials, pricing the owner has not given) is allowed, but it is written as obvious sample content, carries a small "Sample" label on the page, and is listed under `placeholders`. Never invent customer logos, user counts, ratings, certifications or security claims.
- Prices the owner typed stay exactly as typed. Calculated amounts (yearly price after a discount, per-month equivalent) are whole dollars rounded down, and each price is defined once and derived everywhere else.
- The brief decides the words, the look and the sections. These specs decide how everything behaves.
- When the brief is silent, choose a simple default and list it in AGENT_LOG.md under "Decisions on ambiguous points".
- Quiet by default: no pop-ups, no auto-playing media, no fake urgency.
