# SaaS product site: what this kind of product is

Read this first. This pack describes a one-page marketing site for a software product. It is not an online store and not a waitlist test. Where any instruction or example says "store" or "shopper", read "site" and "visitor".

## What it is

A single public page made of blocks in a default order: hero with an email box, a product visual, three feature pills, how it works, (fintech only) a trust block, pricing, testimonials, a closing call to action, and a footer. The visitor leaves their email to get started. There is a minimal owner area to read those emails and nothing else: the page's words are not edited in a screen. Changes to the page are made by asking the builder.

## Who it is for

A founder or a small team that needs a credible product page quickly, in the style of the best software and fintech sites, without a designer. Usually technical enough to judge the result and short of time.

## What good looks like

- A visitor understands what the product does in about five seconds and can leave their email in under ten, on a phone.
- The page looks like a real company: light, spacious, one clear accent, large type, soft cards, a real footer.
- Every email is stored once, with where the visitor came from.
- Nothing on the page is invented. Prices, quotes, logos, numbers, certifications and security claims come from the owner, or the block is left out. A product visual is built from the owner's own words and labelled as a concept when it is not the real product.

## What it is not

No payments, no visitor accounts, no sending email in this version, no blog, no multi-page site, no analytics, no page editor in an owner screen.

## Principles

- The page is blocks in an order. A block with no content from the owner is left out, never filled with sample text.
- The brief decides the words, the look and the form. These specs decide how everything behaves.
- When the brief is silent, choose a simple default and list it in AGENT_LOG.md under "Decisions on ambiguous points".
- Quiet by default: no pop-ups, no auto-playing media, no fake urgency, no invented proof.
